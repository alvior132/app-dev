About the Series
SpongeBob SquarePants** is a funny animated series about a cheerful sea sponge who lives in a pineapple under the sea. He works at the Krusty Krab and enjoys spending time with his friends.

 Main Characters
- SpongeBob – A happy and hardworking sea sponge.
- Patrick Star – SpongeBob's funny and loyal best friend.
- Squidward – SpongeBob's grumpy neighbor.
- Sandy Cheeks – A smart squirrel who lives underwater.
  Mr. Krabs – The owner of the Krusty Krab.

Why I Like It
I like SpongeBob SquarePants because it is funny and entertaining. It always makes me laugh, and I enjoy the friendship and adventures of the characters.
